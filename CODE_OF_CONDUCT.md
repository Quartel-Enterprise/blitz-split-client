# Code of conduct

Blitz Split (Bora Rachar in Portuguese) is built in the open, and everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull
requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to make that a welcoming place.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to Kotlin Multiplatform, SwiftUI or React.
- Disagree about code and product choices with arguments, not with people.
- Give and take review comments gracefully: a review is about the change, never about its author.
- Write in English in issues and pull requests, so everyone can follow. If English is not your first language,
  that is fine: say it your way and we will work it out together.
- Respect people's time and privacy: don't share someone's personal information without their permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address or an email, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every space of the project (this repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions
and reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainers:

- Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request, which reaches the repository's admins; or
- Open a private report from the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), starting its title with
  "Conduct:". It is the repository's only private channel, so we use it for conduct reports too: only the
  maintainers see it, and it is never published as a security advisory.

Every report is reviewed and answered as quickly as we can, and the maintainers keep the reporter's identity and the
details of the report private.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what action to take, depending on how serious the behavior was:

1. **Correction**: a private, written warning, explaining why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be
   asked for.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and no interaction with the people involved for a
   period of time.
3. **Temporary ban**: no interaction of any kind with the project for a period of time.
4. **Permanent ban**: for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards or disparagement of a group of
   people.
