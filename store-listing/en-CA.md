## Description

Blitz Split is the easy way to split bills with friends, roommates, family or your travel group. Add what each person paid and the app works out who owes whom, in each group and across all of them.

SPLIT ANY BILL, YOUR WAY
• Split equally, by exact amounts, percentages, shares or each person's income
• Split by items: give each dish to whoever had it, with the service charge and the cover included
• More than one person paid? Add several payers to the same bill

SCAN THE RECEIPT
• Take a photo of the receipt and the items, prices and total are filled in for you
• Attach photos to a bill and leave comments, so nobody has to ask what it was

SEE WHO OWES WHOM
• One balance per group, and one with each person you share expenses with
• Simplify debts to settle up with the fewest payments
• Record payments in cash, by bank transfer or any other way. Blitz Split doesn't move money: you pay each other as you always do and mark it as paid

MADE FOR TRIPS, HOMES AND EVENTS
• Groups for a trip, the cottage, your home, a party or anything else, each with its own photo
• Every bill in its own currency, with a separate balance per currency
• Categories, search and statistics show where the money went
• Export a group's expenses to CSV or Excel

START IN SECONDS
• No account needed to start: open the app and create a group
• Invite people with a link or a QR code, or add them by name if they don't have the app
• Sign in with Google or Apple to keep everything in sync across your phone, tablet, computer and the web

WORKS OFFLINE
• Add bills and payments without a connection: everything syncs when you're back online
• An activity feed shows what changed in each group

Free, with no ads. In light and dark mode.
